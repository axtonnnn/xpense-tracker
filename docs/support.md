# Xpense support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days.

If it's about AI scans or Xpense Pro, please include your **Account ID**. You'll find it at the bottom of **Settings** (tap your initial on the dashboard, or **Settings** in the sidebar on iPad and Mac).

## Common questions

### How do I use Xpense on my iPad or Mac?

Install Xpense from the App Store on that device and sign in the same way you did on your phone: with Apple, or with a code sent to the same email. Your entries, repeats and settings come across within a few seconds. Receipt photos stay on the device that took them.

### How do I get Xpense Pro on a new device?

Sign in to the same Xpense account and Pro is there. If it isn't, open **Settings → More → Restore purchases** while signed in to the same Apple ID (or Google account) you subscribed with. The Pro screen has the same button.

### I didn't get the sign-in code

Check your spam or junk folder, and that the address is spelled right. Codes can take a minute to arrive. After a minute you can ask for a new one; only the newest code works.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, open the **Settings** app, tap your name, then **Subscriptions → Xpense Pro → Cancel Subscription**. With Pro active, **Manage subscription** in Xpense's settings takes you there too. On Android, use **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**. Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

### How do I get a refund?

Apple handles App Store refunds at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). Google handles Google Play refunds through your Google Play order history.

### Where is my data? Who can see it?

Your entries are kept on your devices and in your private Xpense account, so they sync between your devices. Only your account can read them. Receipt photos stay on the device that took them. The [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) has the details.

### How do I delete my account?

Open **Settings → Delete account** in the app. It deletes your account and everything in it, on every device. If you subscribe to Pro, cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account too.

### Is my data used to train AI?

No. Receipt photos and questions are sent to the AI only to answer that request, and only after you allow AI features. We don't keep them, and they aren't used to train AI models. The [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) names the service that runs the AI. You can switch AI off in **Settings → On this device**.

### Why does reading a receipt take up to a minute?

The AI checks every line, the totals and the date before it answers, so you have less to fix. Keep Xpense open while it reads. A very long receipt may need two photos.

### The AI got something wrong. What now?

Everything is editable before you save, including each item's need or want. You can also change it later: open the entry in **History**.

### How many AI scans do I get?

Every account gets {{FREE_TRIAL_SCANS}} free scans. Xpense Pro includes up to {{PRO_MONTHLY_SCANS}} scans, {{PRO_MONTHLY_CHECKS}} "Should I buy this?" checks and {{PRO_MONTHLY_REVIEWS}} monthly reviews per calendar month. A photo the AI can't read as a receipt or bill doesn't use one up.

### How does the savings goal work?

Tell Xpense what you're saving for, what it costs and how much you set aside each month. That set-aside is already kept out of what's safe to spend, so moving it into savings changes nothing. Add it on the goal when you do (the dashboard asks once at the start of each month). Adding more than your set-aside comes out of what's safe to spend this month, and taking money out puts it back. When you reach the goal and buy the thing, tap **I bought it**: it's paid from your savings, so it isn't counted as spending.

### Can I lock the app?

Yes. Turn on **Settings → On this device → Lock with Face ID**. If Face ID doesn't recognise you, your device passcode opens it.
